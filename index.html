the problem · how it begins

but why? why do we make these decisions in the first place?

the answer is unexpected impulses — mostly from social feeds. thousands of posts a day, overstimulating you with things you don't need, but still making you feel like you're missing out.

the trap

a cardigan you didn't plan for. a gadget that looked useful at 11pm. a drop that screamed, "if you don't get me, you'll regret it forever." before you know it, you're shopping frantically — and the platform makes it worse: discounts, influencer pushes, bundles, BNPL.

"I wasted $78.49 for no reason."

the loop

you didn't set out to blow money. breathing exercises, frozen cards, telling yourself you'll be better — it works for a few weeks. then it's midnight, Cheryl's selling a portable fan with detachable flowers, and checkout is one tap away.

there's not enough friction to ask whether you actually need this, cascading into a vicious cycle of regret & overspending.
